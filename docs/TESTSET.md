# The ten-park test set

Written 2026-10-22, when Timothy set the direction in `INTENT.md`: a guide to
each park that you can trust enough to walk by, proven on ten Connecticut
state parks before it goes wide.

- **The list:** `data/testset.json` (hand-written; says why each park is in it)
- **The guides:** `data/guides/<id>.json` (generated by `makeguides.py`)
- **The numbers:** `data/guides/audit.json` (generated, every run)
- **To look at one:** `python3 tools/guideview.py data/guides/<id>.json -o out.png`
- **The check:** `tools/isotest/guide.mjs`

Everything below was measured on 2026-10-22. Rebuild and re-read before
quoting any of it later; the sources move.

---

## What a guide is

One file per park, baked once, read by the 3D viewer:

- **Boundary** from CT DEEP's property layer, the boundary of record. All ten
  are a single DEEP property, so there was no choosing between sources.
- **Trails as a graph**: junctions and the stretches between them, each with
  its name, its blaze, its surface, its length and its climb. DEEP's trail
  layer wins where it has a trail; OpenStreetMap fills in only where DEEP has
  nothing, and stays labelled as OSM's; walkable roads join stretches that
  would otherwise not connect, labelled as roads.
- **Ways in**: DEEP's own access points, plus every place a trail meets a
  public road. A road point says where the trail reaches the road. It does
  not claim there is parking.
- **Features**: summits and falls from the USGS gazetteer, towers, viewpoints,
  toilets and the rest from OSM, each with its elevation.

Climb comes from the AWS terrain tiles (USGS 3DEP underneath). Checked against
published figures: Cobble Mountain 1,377 ft baked vs 1,380 published; Talcott
Mountain 944 vs 950; Sleeping Giant's highest node 734 vs Mount Carmel's 739.

## Fixed on 2026-10-22

All in `data/verified.json`, each cited; they take effect on the next publish.

1. **Ten state parks were classed "Boat Launch / Water Access"**: Bluff Point,
   Burr Pond, Haddam Meadows, Higganum Reservoir, Lake Waramaug, Mount Tom,
   Squantz Pond, Stillwater Pond, Ross Pond and Stoddard Hill. Cause: the DEEP
   boat launch record wins the dedupe merge and keeps its own subtype.
   `dedupe.py` already refuses to *name* a park after a facility inside it
   (that bug hit Lake Waramaug once), but subtype has no such guard. Each was
   checked against DEEP's property layer before correcting. Only Squantz Pond
   is in the test set; the other nine were found on the way and are the same
   bug.
2. **Hammonasset Beach State Park did not exist as a record.** It had been
   merged *into* the Natural Area Preserve beside it and renamed after it,
   because the curated park record carried no acreage and the tie-break fell
   to "the longer name wins". `dedupe.py` has a comment saying this exact case
   was fixed; it came back through the missing-acreage path. Renamed, reclassed,
   given DEEP's 581 acres. The test set now points at that record
   (`f999a0ef6c`), not the boat launch it pointed at first.
3. **Per-park facts for all ten**, from each park's page on ctparks.com
   (DEEP's own state parks site), replacing the statewide defaults: hours,
   out-of-state parking fees, dogs, swimming, closures. Every quote was
   checked against the live page by a script, and the ones the corrections
   rest on were re-checked by hand. Four parks charge **no** parking fee at
   all (Gillette Castle, Devil's Hopyard, Macedonia Brook, Mashamoquet Brook)
   and now read `free` instead of `parking`.
4. **The 7 ft leash rule is right, and now cited properly.** No park page
   states it, which looked like an unsupported claim on all 523 Connecticut
   state places.
   It is in the regulation, RCSA § 23-4-1, which also bans pets at Squantz
   Pond from April 15 to September 30, matching the park page.

Measured: a dry-run publish with and without these entries differs on
exactly the 19 intended records and no others.

## Found and open

- **The committed dataset is not at a dedupe fixed point.** Any publish,
  with or without these entries, merges 150 records and moves verified parks
  from 18,737 to 18,672. Pre-existing; not caused by today's work. Worth
  understanding before the next refresh, because "publishing twice gave two
  answers" is a failure this project has had before.
- **The dedupe subtype rule itself** is unfixed. The ten corrections hold, but
  the next boat launch to win a merge will do the same. The fix belongs in
  `merge_into` (a facility subtype yields to a property subtype) and wants a
  full refresh to measure, which is why it was not done blind.
- **Boat launches carry their park's acreage.** The Squantz Cove launch reads
  163 acres; the Hammonasset launch read 577. Parcel-under-centroid again (the
  failure table in `CLAUDE.md` has this row twice already).
- **DEEP's trail layer holds pavement.** 22 km of Hammonasset's 29 km of DEEP
  "trail" lies on OSM roads; it is unnamed bituminous. The guide marks it
  `onRoad` and the directions call it the park road. Gillette, Macedonia,
  Chatfield and Squantz have 2 to 7 km each of the same.
- **Blazes are often unrecorded.** Hammonasset and Gillette record none at
  all (plausibly true of a beach and of railroad-bed paths, but unverified);
  Squantz 43%, Kent Falls 47%. A route over unblazed trail says so.
- **Two parks have almost no named features**: Mashamoquet (2) and Squantz (2).
  The Wolf Den, Mashamoquet's destination, is not in any source this reads.
  That is the clearest case for hand-added features.
- **Talcott Mountain is four separate networks.** Two are other parcels 800 m
  and further away; that is the land, not a fault. The Heublein Tower route is
  on the main piece and works.
- **Short gaps a person should look at**, from `audit.json` `graph.gaps`:
  Sleeping Giant at 41.4226, -72.8914 (31 m), Squantz Pond at 41.5088, -73.4703
  (70 m), Hammonasset at 41.2737, -72.5636 (60 m). A gap that short is usually
  a bridge or a crossing nobody mapped. If one is real, it goes in by hand.

## When is a park's guide done

Proposed, for Timothy to change:

1. Boundary, name, class, acreage and fee agree with DEEP. *(all ten now)*
2. The network is one piece, or every other piece is a separate parcel.
   *(eight of ten; Hammonasset and Sleeping Giant have short gaps to check)*
3. Every way in reaches the network. *(all ten)*
4. The park's own destination is a destination: Sleeping Giant's summit,
   Talcott's tower, Kent Falls' falls, Gillette's castle, the Wolf Den.
   *(not yet checked park by park; the Wolf Den is missing)*
5. Someone who has walked it agrees with the directions. *(none yet; this is
   the test the data cannot pass by itself)*
